2.Data we collect

SourceWhat we receive
Enquiry formYour name, email address, company or tuition centre name, phone number if you give it, the topic you choose, your answers to the topic questions (such as your role or number of students) and your message
Email and WhatsAppYour name, email address or phone number, and whatever you choose to write to us
Visiting the siteStandard technical data such as your IP address and browser type, which our hosting and font providers receive when your browser loads the page (section 4)

Please do not send us sensitive data, such as NRIC numbers or health information, through the form or chat.

3.How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and route it to the right person.
  • To follow up on a demo, partnership or consultancy discussion you asked about.
  • To keep a record of our business correspondence.
  • To protect the website from spam and abuse.

We will only send you news or marketing if you ask us to, and you can stop it at any time.

4.Service providers

We may also disclose data where the law requires it.

5.Cookies and tracking

4abnc.com does not set cookies and does not use analytics, advertising or social media tracking. The Where2Charge website and app use analytics, as described in their own policy.

6.How long we keep it

DataHow long we keep it
Enquiries and correspondence2 years after our last contact with you, unless you become a customer
Customer correspondenceWhile we work together, then 5 years for legal and accounting records
Form submissions held by FormspreeDeleted from Formspree within 90 days, after they reach our inbox

7.Security

The website is served over HTTPS, the form is protected against spam, and access to enquiries is limited to the staff who handle them. No system is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data and will notify you and the authorities where the PDPA requires it.

8.Your rights

Under the PDPA you may ask to see the personal data we hold about you, correct it, or withdraw your consent to our using it. You can also ask us to delete your enquiry. Write to us (section 11) and we will respond within 30 days.

9.Overseas transfers

Formspree, GitHub and Google process data outside Singapore, including in the United States. We use providers that protect data to a standard comparable to the PDPA.

10.Changes to this policy

We may update this policy and will change the "Last updated" date above when we do.
